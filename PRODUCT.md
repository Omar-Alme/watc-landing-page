## Platform

web

## Users

Importers, wholesalers, brands and startups outside China (Europe, the Gulf, North America) who want to buy from Chinese factories but fear scams, quality failures and miscommunication. Many read English, a large share read Arabic (the site is bilingual EN/AR with RTL).

## Product Purpose

World Associates Trading Company (WATC Global) is a sourcing and export agent based in Guangzhou. It finds verified factories, inspects goods on site, and ships containers worldwide. The landing page exists to turn a cautious visitor into a conversation: a consultation inquiry (form) or a WhatsApp message, given equal weight.

## Positioning

A company legally registered in Guangzhou since 9 December 2004, with an office on Yanjiang Middle Road (Yuexiu District), whose people physically visit factories. Anyone can verify the registration on China's national credit registry (gsxt.gov.cn) with the unified social credit code 9144010176950411XG.

## Operating Context

The seven-step order flow: inquiry → quote → sample; customer pays WATC, WATC pays factory; production follow-ups; packing and customs; container loading and export documents; bill of lading; final document handoff. Weekly updates and inspection photos during orders. Languages: English, Chinese, Arabic.
